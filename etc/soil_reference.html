Reading these numbers

  • The parameters are colour coordinates, not a soil assay. humus is exactly 1 - brightness, so any dark soil reads as humus-rich whatever its actual carbon; iron is chroma along the empirical soil colour arc, so a buff carbonate with no free iron still reads as moderately “ferruginous”.
  • Fit quality. Median swatch ΔEab against the measured soil is 1.1; the worst entry is 6.3 (Red earth, Hubei). Full-band RMSE runs 0.027 median — the residual is almost entirely SWIR clay and carbonate absorption bands, which a four-parameter colour model cannot carry.
  • Gamut edges. The dry model spans roughly 5–46% average visible reflectance. The strongest natural reds saturate iron and aridity at 1.0, and pure white sands, gypsum and alumina sit above the brightness ceiling — presets near those corners are directionally right but not exact.
  • Humus-rich soils are the weak spot. Of the eight database soils with OM ≥ 70 g/kg only one fits inside ΔE 2.5; the rest miss by 3–5. Use the organic presets here rather than extrapolating past them.
